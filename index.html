Environmental impact

Textile waste is a solvable problem

Every garment that gets a second life keeps usable fabric out of landfill and reduces the demand for virgin fibre.

92M tons Textile waste produced globally each year
< 15% Of discarded clothing is currently reused or recycled
2,700 L Water to produce a single new cotton shirt
1 garment Kept in use can save several kilograms of CO₂

🌱 Sustainability Tips

  • 🧺 Wash and dry clothes before scheduling a pickup — clean textiles are far easier to reuse.
  • 👕 Items in “Like New” or “Good” condition are best for donation; torn or faded items suit recycling.
  • 📦 Pack pieces in one sturdy bag and label it with your request code.

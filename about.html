About ThreadAI

An AI-powered clothing reuse and recycling platform — built as a Full Stack Development micro project to make responsible clothing disposal the easy choice.

Our purpose

Clothes should never go to waste quietly

Most of us own more clothes than we wear. When they stop being useful, they are usually thrown away — even though wearable textiles could be donated and worn again, and unwearable ones can still be shredded into new fibre.

ThreadAI closes that gap. A user uploads a photo of an unwanted garment, the system recommends the best next step — donate, reuse, or recycle — and a doorstep pickup is scheduled. An admin then manages each request until the pickup is completed.

🌱 This is a student micro project.

This is a full-stack prototype: a Node.js/Express backend with a real database, user authentication, and live Google Gemini AI clothing analysis — all running end to end.

What we stand for

Three goals behind every request

🗑️➡️👗

Reduce Textile Waste

Keep usable clothing out of landfills and incinerators by giving every garment a planned next step instead of a rushed throw-away.

🔁

Clothing Reuse

Wearable items are routed to donation so they can be worn again — the most impactful option for both people and the planet.

♻️

Responsible Recycling

Garments beyond reuse are sent for fibre recovery, turning old fabric into new material instead of waste.
